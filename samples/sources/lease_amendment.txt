FIRST AMENDMENT TO COMMERCIAL LEASE

This First Amendment (the "Amendment") is made on September 2, 2025 between Granite Harbor Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Fernwood Legal Group PLLC ("Tenant") and amends the Commercial Lease dated January 15, 2025 for the 12th floor of 410 Market Street, Austin, Texas.

Section 1. Expansion Premises. Effective November 1, 2025, the Premises are expanded to include an additional 1,350 rentable square feet on the 12th floor (the "Expansion Space").

Section 2. Rent for Expansion Space. Tenant shall pay additional base rent of $6,075 per month for the Expansion Space, subject to the same three percent (3%) annual increase as the original Premises.

Section 3. Parking. Landlord shall provide Tenant with six (6) reserved parking spaces in the building garage at no additional charge for the remainder of the term.

Section 4. Ratification. Except as amended here, the Lease remains in full force and effect. Signed copies of this Amendment were exchanged by scanned email.
