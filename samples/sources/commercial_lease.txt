COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement (the "Lease") is entered into on January 15, 2025 between Granite Harbor Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Fernwood Legal Group PLLC ("Tenant").

Section 1. Premises. Landlord leases to Tenant approximately 4,200 rentable square feet on the 12th floor of the building located at 410 Market Street, Austin, Texas (the "Premises").

Section 2. Term. The term of this Lease is five (5) years, commencing on April 1, 2025 and ending on March 31, 2030, unless terminated earlier as provided herein.

Section 3. Base Rent. Tenant shall pay base rent of $18,900 per month, due on the first day of each calendar month. Base rent increases by three percent (3%) on each anniversary of the commencement date. Rent not received within five (5) days of the due date incurs a late fee of five percent (5%) of the overdue amount.

Section 4. Security Deposit. Tenant shall deposit $37,800 with Landlord as security for Tenant's performance. Landlord shall return the deposit, less lawful deductions, within thirty (30) days after the Lease ends.

Section 5. Use. Tenant shall use the Premises only for general office purposes, including the practice of law, and for no other purpose without Landlord's prior written consent.

Section 6. Maintenance and Repairs. Landlord is responsible for the roof, structure, elevators and building systems. Tenant is responsible for interior repairs and for damage caused by Tenant or its invitees.

Section 7. Assignment and Subletting. Tenant may not assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed.

Section 8. Early Termination. Tenant may terminate this Lease effective on the last day of the thirty-sixth (36th) month by giving Landlord at least nine (9) months prior written notice and paying a termination fee equal to four (4) months of then-current base rent.

Section 9. Default. If Tenant fails to pay rent within ten (10) days after written notice of non-payment, or fails to cure any other default within thirty (30) days after written notice, Landlord may terminate this Lease and pursue all remedies available at law.

Section 10. Governing Law. This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Texas.
