EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement (the "Agreement") is made effective as of February 3, 2025 between Brightwater Robotics Inc., a Massachusetts corporation (the "Company"), and Jordan Ellis (the "Employee").

Section 1. Position. The Company employs the Employee as Senior Corporate Counsel, reporting to the General Counsel. The Employee shall devote full business time and efforts to the Company.

Section 2. Compensation. The Company shall pay the Employee an annual base salary of $210,000, payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices. The Employee is eligible for an annual performance bonus of up to twenty percent (20%) of base salary, determined at the Company's discretion.

Section 3. Equity. Subject to board approval, the Employee will receive an option to purchase 40,000 shares of common stock, vesting over four (4) years with a one (1) year cliff.

Section 4. Benefits. The Employee may participate in the Company's health, dental and retirement plans and receives twenty (20) days of paid time off per year.

Section 5. At-Will Employment. Employment is at will. Either party may terminate the employment at any time, with or without cause, upon two (2) weeks written notice.

Section 6. Severance. If the Company terminates the Employee without cause, the Company shall pay severance equal to three (3) months of base salary, conditioned on the Employee signing a general release of claims.

Section 7. Confidentiality and Inventions. The Employee shall not disclose the Company's confidential information during or after employment, and assigns to the Company all inventions conceived during employment that relate to the Company's business.

Section 8. Non-Solicitation. For twelve (12) months after employment ends, the Employee shall not solicit any Company employee or customer to leave the Company.

Section 9. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts.
